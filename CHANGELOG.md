# Changelog: MCA Inclusive Expressions (MC 26.3)

### Changed
- **MC 26.3 Parity Polish**: Updated to align with DasikLibrary 1.9.2 and Minecraft 26.3 rendering pipeline.

### Added
- **Minecraft 26.3 Port**: Complete port of MCA Inclusive Expressions to Minecraft 26.3.

- **Full Massive Scale Preservation**: Preserved extended 4.44x scale limits, 6-axis position controls, 3D rotation, movement jiggle physics, and torso Z-clamping.
